# Give me money

One button that pays the creator (irfani) 10 Möbius credits. Built to test
credit payouts: the creator keeps 8 credits and Möbius keeps 2 per press.

The price is published from the creator's Möbius as `give-me-money/give`.
Each press makes one charge from the buyer's own credits through the app's
service; Möbius asks the buyer to confirm with Pay before anything moves.
